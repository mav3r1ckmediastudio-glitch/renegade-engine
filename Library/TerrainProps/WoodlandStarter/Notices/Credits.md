# Woodland Starter credits

Each entry in catalog.json links its source record with creators, source URLs and permissions. The original upstream files and tree recipes are in Originals.

* Poly Haven supplied the CC0 fern, rock and stump scans. Individual source records credit the artists.
* tevy supplied the CC0 woodland assets and their original detail levels. The two-stem birch uses the same silver birch in two arrangements.
* Daniel Greenheck's EZ-Tree generated the meadow oak, veteran oak, young pine and standing dead oak. See the enclosed MIT license and the upstream texture attribution. The oak and pine foliage is MIT licensed. ambientCG supplied CC0 bark.
* Renegade-authored starter artwork. Supplied for inclusion with Renegade Engine and in games made with it. The pebble cluster uses the CC0 Poly Haven rock.

All lengths are in metres. The catalog lists the near model, any supplied lower detail meshes, ground pivot and measured size for each item.
