## How to read historical material

Current guidance may retain labelled historical subsections. Dated plans, gate
specifications and receipts preserve their original baseline and acceptance scope.
Their old “pending”, “not implemented” or “do not merge” text is not automatically
today’s status. CURRENT_STATUS.md resolves integration; it does not erase failures
or complete independent release gates. The July MASTER_PLAN is original planning.

The projectile manual remains a draft with its own publication limits. Local QA
preset catalogs and proof projects are not automatically installed/shipped assets.
docs/evidence files remain immutable scoped receipts; this pass does not relabel
them as independent acceptance.
