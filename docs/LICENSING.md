# Licensing and Third-Party Notices

## Wicked Engine

Wicked Engine is licensed under the MIT License. Its copyright and licence text
must remain in copies or substantial portions. The complete upstream licence is
available at `WickedEngine/LICENSE.txt` after submodule checkout.

Wicked also maintains `WickedEngine/third_party_software.txt`. Release packaging
must include all notices relevant to the components and assets actually shipped.

## Renegade-authored code and assets

A project-wide licence has not yet been selected. Public visibility does not by
itself grant a reuse licence. Do not add a root `LICENSE` or describe Renegade as
open source until the owner records that decision.

## Release gate

Before distributing an editor, runtime, template, or sample:

1. Inventory included Wicked and third-party components.
2. Copy required licence and notice text into the package.
3. Verify fonts, sample assets, codecs, and optional libraries separately.
4. Record the notice bundle in release tests.
5. Obtain legal review where commercial distribution warrants it.
